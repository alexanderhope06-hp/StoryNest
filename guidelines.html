Community Guidelines

Creating a safe and welcoming space for all storytellers and readers.

StoryNest is a community of storytellers and story lovers. These guidelines help us maintain a creative, respectful, and safe environment for everyone.

🤝 Be Kind and Respectful

  • Treat others with kindness and respect
  • Constructive criticism is welcome, bullying is not
  • Respect different opinions and perspectives
  • No hate speech, discrimination, or harassment

📝 Content Guidelines

Writing & Stories:

  • Write original work that you own the rights to
  • No plagiarism or copying others' work
  • Use appropriate tags for mature content
  • Content must be legal and not harmful

Prohibited Content:

  • ❌ Explicit sexual content without proper tagging
  • ❌ Graphic violence without warning
  • ❌ Content promoting illegal activities
  • ❌ Hate speech or discrimination
  • ❌ Harassment or bullying
  • ❌ Spam or misleading content

📖 Reader Guidelines

  • Be respectful of authors and their work
  • Provide constructive feedback when requested
  • Don't spoil stories for others without warning
  • Report content that violates guidelines

✍️ Writer Guidelines

  • Write original content that you own
  • Use appropriate tags and ratings
  • Engage respectfully with readers
  • Don't use manipulative tactics for reads
  • Be honest about your work

🚫 What Not to Do

  • Don't impersonate other users or authors
  • Don't share others' private information
  • Don't try to hack or disrupt the platform
  • Don't post spam or advertisements
  • Don't exploit the monetization system

📊 Enforcement

  • Violations may result in content removal
  • Repeat violations may lead to account suspension
  • Serious violations may result in permanent ban
  • We reserve the right to take appropriate action

📋 Reporting

If you see content that violates these guidelines:

  • Use the report feature (when available)
  • Contact us at abuse@storynest.com
  • Provide details about the violation
  • We will investigate and take action

🤗 Together We Build Community

StoryNest is built by and for the community. Every user helps create a welcoming environment for storytellers and readers alike. Thank you for being part of our community!
