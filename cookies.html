Cookie Policy

How StoryNest uses cookies to improve your experience.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

Essential Cookies

  • Authentication: Keep you logged in
  • Security: Protect against fraud
  • Session Management: Track your active session

Preference Cookies

  • Theme: Remember your light/dark mode preference
  • Font Size: Remember your reading preferences
  • Language: Remember your language choice

Analytics Cookies

  • Usage: Understand how you use StoryNest
  • Performance: Identify and fix issues
  • Improvement: Make the platform better

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

Disabling cookies may affect some features of StoryNest.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:

  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns
  • Advertising: Future ad services (when implemented)
  • Social Media: If you share content

Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Last until you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Last for a set period (up to 1 year)

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Check back for changes.
