Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and protect your privacy.

1. Introduction

At StoryNest, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, password
  • Profile Information: Bio, avatar, social media links
  • Content: Novels, chapters, comments, feedback
  • Communication: Messages, support requests

We automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, reading progress
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type
  • Location: Approximate location (country level only)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve: Deliver our services, fix bugs, and improve user experience
  • To personalize: Recommend stories, remember preferences
  • To communicate: Send updates, respond to inquiries
  • To analyze: Understand usage patterns and improve content
  • To protect: Prevent fraud, enforce terms of service

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly agree
  • With service providers: To help us operate (e.g., hosting, analytics)
  • For legal reasons: To comply with laws or protect rights
  • Business transfers: If StoryNest is acquired or merged

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Export your content
  • Object: Opt out of certain processing

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@storynest.com

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • 🔒 Encryption: All data transmitted securely via HTTPS
  • 🔐 Authentication: Secure login with password hashing
  • 🛡️ Access Control: Limited internal access to user data
  • 📊 Monitoring: Regular security audits and monitoring

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings (e.g., dark mode, font size)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use the platform

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

StoryNest is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or platform notification.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@storynest.com
  • Platform: Contact Form
