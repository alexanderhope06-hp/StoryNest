Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about StoryNest.

📖 For Readers

You can browse novels on the homepage under "Trending" and "New Releases." You can also use the search bar to find specific titles, authors, or genres. Click on any novel card to view its details and start reading.

You can browse and read published novels without an account. However, creating a free account allows you to bookmark novels, save your reading progress, and support authors.

When viewing a novel's details page, click the "Bookmark" button. You'll need to be logged in to save bookmarks. Your bookmarks will appear in your profile once the feature is fully implemented.

Currently, StoryNest requires an internet connection to read novels. We're working on an offline reading feature for future updates.

✍️ For Writers

First, create a free account and log in. Then visit your Author Dashboard and click "Add New Novel." Fill in the details, add chapters, and publish when you're ready. It's that simple!

StoryNest is building a monetization system where authors earn revenue from ad impressions and reader engagement. Stay tuned for updates on when monetization features will be fully available.

Yes! You can publish chapters one at a time. Readers will see new chapters appear as you add them. You can also save chapters as drafts and publish them later.

You retain full ownership of your stories. You're giving StoryNest a license to host and display your content, but you can delete it or publish elsewhere at any time. See our Terms of Service for details.

🔧 Technical Support

On the login page, look for a "Forgot Password" link. Click it and enter your email address. We'll send you instructions to reset your password. If you don't see the link, please contact support.

Common issues include: incorrect email/password, unconfirmed email address, or account suspension. Try resetting your password or contact support if the problem persists.

To delete your account, please contact our support team at support@storynest.com. Include your account email and we'll process the deletion. Note that this is permanent.

Yes! We use industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication, and regular security audits. See our Privacy Policy for detailed information about how we protect your data.

💰 Monetization & Earnings

Earnings will begin when the monetization system is fully launched. We'll notify all authors when this happens. In the meantime, focus on building your readership!

Earnings depend on several factors: number of readers, engagement levels, ad impressions, and revenue share terms. We're working to create a fair and transparent system for all authors.

Withdrawal features are currently in development. Once available, you'll be able to request withdrawals through your Author Dashboard. Stay tuned for updates!
